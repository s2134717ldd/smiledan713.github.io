Welcome! I'm Dandan Liu (Ivy), a PhD candidate in the Department of Media and Communication Studies, Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences at Universiti Malaya, Malaysia.

Research Interests
======
My research focuses on the dynamic interplay between digital media and contemporary communication practices, with particular emphasis on:
- **Social Media**: Examining social media platforms, user behavior, and digital content creation
- **Crisis Communication**: Investigating communication strategies and information dissemination during crises
- **Computational Communication**: Applying computational methods to analyze large-scale communication data
- **Digital Discourse and Media Dynamics**: Understanding how digital discourse shapes and is shaped by media ecosystems

Current Research
======
My doctoral research centers on digital discourse and media dynamics in the context of social media and crisis communication. I employ both qualitative and computational approaches to understand how information flows, how communities form and respond during crises, and how digital platforms mediate public discourse. I am particularly interested in leveraging computational methods to analyze patterns in digital communication and their implications for society.

Recent Publications
======
I have published in several prestigious international journals, including:
- *Information, Communication and Society* - exploring digital identity and social media practices
- *International Journal of STEM Education* - investigating AI-assisted learning and educational technology
- *BMC Nursing* - examining communication in healthcare contexts

My interdisciplinary work demonstrates the application of communication theories across diverse contexts, from rural social media creators to educational technology and healthcare communication.

Academic Background
======
I hold both my bachelor's and master's degrees in Journalism and Communication from Fudan University's School of Journalism (QS Top 31, Chinese 985&211 Project Elite Institution). My bachelor thesis on mobile news app usage received the Second Prize of People's Daily Online Excellent Paper Award. I am currently pursuing my PhD at Universiti Malaya, focusing on advanced research in media and communication studies with emphasis on computational methods and digital discourse analysis.

Professional Experience
======
Before pursuing my doctoral studies, I worked in marketing operations at Shanghai Kaijie Education and Technology Co., Ltd., where I gained experience in digital marketing and educational technology. I also served as a Marketing Manager at China UnionPay Merchant Services Co., Ltd., bringing practical industry experience to complement my academic research. This combination of industry and academic experience informs my research approach and understanding of real-world communication challenges.
