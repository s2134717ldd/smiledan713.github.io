\block{Research Profile}
My research examines public discourse, human--AI communication, and trust on digital platforms. Combining communication theory with qualitative and computational approaches, I study how platform interventions shape interaction and how institutions frame public health crises.
\par{\small\textbf{Themes:} Human--AI interaction \sep Platform governance and trust \sep Crisis framing}

\block{Selected Research and Contributions}
{\small First-author research led by Dandan Liu; detailed roles below follow published contribution statements where available. IDs refer to the complete list.}\par
\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\textbf{From Solo Post to Shared Space [J1]}\\
{\small \emph{ACM Transactions on Social Computing} (2026). Led the research on how a public LLM agent reshapes human-to-human conversation. The study uses reply-network analysis, matching, and doubly robust estimation to examine interaction structure on a social platform.}

\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\textbf{\textquotedbl{}Fairness Labels\textquotedbl{} in Feeds [J2]}\\
{\small \emph{Social Media + Society} (2026). Led research using an online randomized experiment to examine creators' fairness and legitimacy judgments. Documented contributions include study design, software, investigation, data curation, formal analysis, visualization, and drafting and revising the manuscript.}

\Needspace{6\baselineskip}
\textbf{From zero-COVID to global alignment [J5]}\\
{\small \emph{Globalization and Health} (2025), Brief Report. Led the research examining 154 press-conference transcripts through topic modeling, frame analysis, and process tracing. The published statement records equal contributions by all three authors to conception, design, analysis, and writing.}

\Needspace{5\baselineskip}
\textbf{Digital duality [J6]}\\
{\small \emph{Information, Communication \& Society} (2025). Led an interview-based study of how rural housewife Douyin creators negotiate tradition, modernity, and identity.}
