\block{Research Profile}
\begingroup\raggedright
I study how digital platforms shape public discourse and human interaction. Combining qualitative and computational approaches, my research examines social media, crisis communication, and \mbox{Human--AI Interaction}.
\par\endgroup

\block{Research Themes}
\textbf{Digital discourse and crisis communication.} Pandemic communication, transnational influences, and the relationships between government messaging, social media, and public discourse.

\textbf{Human--AI communication.} Public LLM agents, emotional support and authenticity, and the changing structure of human-to-human interaction on social platforms.

\textbf{Platform governance and trust.} Fairness disclosures, content provenance, user choice, and the authority of AI-generated summaries and wearable wellness scores.
